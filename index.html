Welcome to TwoSide Group

Speak with our receptionist right here in your browser. She can answer general questions, take your details for a meeting or a service, and pass everything to the Chairman for follow-up.

Voice calls work best in Chrome on a laptop. A typed option is always available if you prefer not to use the microphone.

Before the call starts

Your speech is transcribed by your browser's speech service — in Chrome, that service is provided by Google. No voice recording is kept by TwoSide Group.

A type-instead path is always available: if the microphone is unavailable or you decline permission, you can type your side of the conversation instead.

The receptionist speaks only after you start or accept a call.
